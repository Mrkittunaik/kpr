Powder Coating & Finishing

Durable, aesthetic surface treatments offering superior corrosion resistance and a lasting finish.

What we offer

A good finish protects the part as well as improving how it looks. Our powder coating gives fabricated components a durable, even surface that stands up to everyday use.

  • Durable, long-lasting finish
  • Corrosion resistance for fabricated parts
  • Aesthetic finishes to suit your product
  • Convenient after cutting, bending and fabrication
  • Suitable for single parts and batches
